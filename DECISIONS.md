# Decisions

Choices made where [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) is silent or ambiguous. Newest last.

| #   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                | Why                                                                                                                                               |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Section 10 open questions use their bracketed defaults: name Crownstack, MIT, orthographic camera at 50° pitch, the king does not attack, no analytics.                                 | Spec default.                                                                                                                                     |
| 2   | TypeScript pinned to 6.0.x rather than 7.x.                                                                                                                                             | `typescript-eslint` 8 does not yet support TS 7.                                                                                                  |
| 3   | Content JSON is imported and bundled into the JS chunks instead of being fetched at runtime.                                                                                            | The spec requires content to be precached; bundling gives that for free, makes bad JSON a build/test failure, and needs no runtime loading state. |
| 4   | PWA icons are generated by `scripts/gen-icons.mjs` and committed.                                                                                                                       | Keeps "no binary source assets" while giving the manifest real PNGs.                                                                              |
| 5   | Fredoka is self-hosted through `@fontsource-variable/fredoka` (OFL).                                                                                                                    | Spec asks for one self-hosted open-licence rounded font.                                                                                          |
| 6   | Hand-rolled ECS rather than `bitecs`.                                                                                                                                                   | Fewer dependencies and full control over determinism and allocation.                                                                              |
| 7   | The camera uses yaw 45°, so the rectangular yard reads as a diamond. Compass names in content are world-space; on screen west is top-left and north is top-right.                       | Matches the spec's camera and the ad's layout (keep top-left, path from the top-right).                                                           |
| 8   | The camera fits the whole level only when that leaves at least 24 CSS px per world unit; on smaller screens it follows the king, clamped to the level's camera bounds.                  | Fitting a 33×29 level on a phone makes characters a few pixels tall.                                                                              |
| 9   | Characters and coins are drawn 1.3–1.5× their collision size.                                                                                                                           | Readability on phones without changing the spec's stats or the 24×18 yard.                                                                        |
| 10  | Pads start drawing coins after a 0.45 s dwell.                                                                                                                                          | Stops the king from paying by accident when running across a pad.                                                                                 |
| 11  | The keep-upgrade pad spends the bank first, then the stack.                                                                                                                             | The spec says it "draws from the bank", but the bank only fills once the stack is full; this keeps the pad usable early.                          |
| 12  | Ground layers are stored as rectangles (`ground.patches`, `blockers.walls`) on the 1-unit grid rather than as per-cell arrays.                                                          | Same information, far smaller and easier to hand-edit.                                                                                            |
| 13  | A level lists which of its map's pads are enabled (and their cost); pad positions live in the map.                                                                                      | Keeps "levels are data" without repeating coordinates in every level.                                                                             |
| 14  | `Stack` and `Pad` are not per-entity components: the stack is a singleton on `world.eco`, pads are a small static list.                                                                 | There is exactly one stack and a handful of pads; SoA columns for them would be empty.                                                            |
| 15  | Enemies chase a king or archer who comes within 2.5 units, and attack whatever standing fence blocks them. After their path's fence falls they go for the nearest tower, then the keep. | Fills in "attack nearest fence/tower/unit" with the simplest rule that needs no pathfinding.                                                      |
| 16  | A destroyed tower frees its plot and re-enables its pad at the current tower price.                                                                                                     | Otherwise a lost tower could never be replaced.                                                                                                   |
| 17  | Retry keeps the attempt number (same seed, same waves); starting a level from a menu bumps it.                                                                                          | SPEC §3.6: "Retry reproduces the waves".                                                                                                          |
| 18  | The 3 ms simulation budget is asserted by a normal unit test (`tests/unit/perf.test.ts`) instead of `vitest bench`.                                                                     | Runs in CI on every push and fails the build on regression; bench mode only reports.                                                              |
| 19  | `?quality=low` (no MSAA, DPR 1, fewer particles) is used by the Playwright tests.                                                                                                       | GitHub runners render WebGL in software; low quality keeps them near 30 fps.                                                                      |
| 20  | Damage popups are pre-rendered per damage value found in `units.json`.                                                                                                                  | One atlas, one draw call, no per-frame canvas work.                                                                                               |
