# Decisions

Choices made where [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) is silent or ambiguous. Newest last.

| #   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Section 10 open questions use their bracketed defaults: name Crownstack, MIT, orthographic camera at 50° pitch, the king does not attack, no analytics.                                                                                                                                                                               | Spec default.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 2   | TypeScript pinned to 6.0.x rather than 7.x.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | `typescript-eslint` 8 does not yet support TS 7.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 3   | Content JSON is imported and bundled into the JS chunks instead of being fetched at runtime.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | The spec requires content to be precached; bundling gives that for free, makes bad JSON a build/test failure, and needs no runtime loading state.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 4   | PWA icons are generated by `scripts/gen-icons.mjs` and committed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Keeps "no binary source assets" while giving the manifest real PNGs.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 5   | Fredoka is self-hosted through `@fontsource-variable/fredoka` (OFL).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Spec asks for one self-hosted open-licence rounded font.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 6   | Hand-rolled ECS rather than `bitecs`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Fewer dependencies and full control over determinism and allocation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 7   | The camera uses yaw 45°, so the rectangular yard reads as a diamond. Compass names in content are world-space; on screen west is top-left and north is top-right.                                                                                                                                                                     | Matches the spec's camera and the ad's layout (keep top-left, path from the top-right).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 8   | The camera fits the whole level only when that leaves at least 24 CSS px per world unit; on smaller screens it follows the king, clamped to the level's camera bounds.                                                                                                                                                                | Fitting a 33×29 level on a phone makes characters a few pixels tall.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 9   | Characters and coins are drawn 1.3–1.5× their collision size.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Readability on phones without changing the spec's stats or the 24×18 yard.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 10  | Pads start drawing coins after a 0.45 s dwell.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Stops the king from paying by accident when running across a pad.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 11  | The keep-upgrade pad spends the bank first, then the stack.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | The spec says it "draws from the bank", but the bank only fills once the stack is full; this keeps the pad usable early.                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 12  | Ground layers are stored as rectangles (`ground.patches`, `blockers.walls`) on the 1-unit grid rather than as per-cell arrays.                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Same information, far smaller and easier to hand-edit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 13  | A level lists which of its map's pads are enabled (and their cost); pad positions live in the map.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Keeps "levels are data" without repeating coordinates in every level.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 14  | `Stack` and `Pad` are not per-entity components: the stack is a singleton on `world.eco`, pads are a small static list.                                                                                                                                                                                                               | There is exactly one stack and a handful of pads; SoA columns for them would be empty.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 15  | Enemies chase a king or archer who comes within 2.5 units, and attack whatever standing fence blocks them. After their path's fence falls they go for the nearest tower, then the keep.                                                                                                                                               | Fills in "attack nearest fence/tower/unit" with the simplest rule that needs no pathfinding.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 16  | A destroyed tower frees its plot and re-enables its pad at the current tower price.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Otherwise a lost tower could never be replaced.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 17  | Retry keeps the attempt number (same seed, same waves); starting a level from a menu bumps it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | SPEC §3.6: "Retry reproduces the waves".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 18  | The 3 ms simulation budget is asserted by a normal unit test (`tests/unit/perf.test.ts`) instead of `vitest bench`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Runs in CI on every push and fails the build on regression; bench mode only reports.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 19  | `?quality=low` (no MSAA, DPR 1, fewer particles) is used by the Playwright tests.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | GitHub runners render WebGL in software; low quality keeps them near 30 fps.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 20  | Damage popups are pre-rendered per damage value found in `units.json`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | One atlas, one draw call, no per-frame canvas work.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 21  | After a pad triggers it takes no more coins until the king steps off it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Otherwise standing still rolls straight into the next, 30% dearer purchase.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 22  | Forged bows are handed to squad archers within 2.2 units automatically, one every 0.15 s; bows left over when every archer is at the level's tier cap expire with the 30 s timer.                                                                                                                                                     | The squad already rings the king at 1.5 units, so "walk into them" is satisfied by returning to the squad.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 23  | The keep upgrade adds +10 carry cap (per spec), +250 keep HP with a full heal, and two recruits.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | The spec gives the upgrade a cost but only one effect, and gives no other way to replace dead archers.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 24  | Squad archers do not collide with their own palisade and regroup on the king if left more than 12 units behind.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | They steer without pathfinding and were stranded outside when the king came back through the gate.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 25  | Tutorial triggers are a small fixed grammar (`start`, `gold>=N`, `wave>=N`, `gearCarried`, `fenceDamaged`, `padPaid:<type>`) checked by the schema; the tutorial runner lives outside `sim/`.                                                                                                                                         | Keeps hints data-driven without putting UI state in the simulation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 26  | Level tuning uses two bots in `tests/bot` (`pnpm bot`): the spec's naive "nearest coin, cheapest pad" bot and a careful one that avoids coins near enemies and retreats when hurt.                                                                                                                                                    | The careful bot stands in for a competent player when checking that a level is winnable.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 27  | Enemy paths run through the fence or gate they name (`targetFence`) and on to the keep. Enemies break whatever standing barrier blocks them, then continue; at the end of the path they attack a tower within 7 units, else the keep.                                                                                                 | With straight-line "go to the keep" movement enemies got stuck on rivers and buildings on the larger maps. Paths give them a route without pathfinding.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 28  | Gates are barrier segments that start open. A gate pad shuts every gate on the map for 20 s (`economy.gateCloseSec`); a closed gate blocks the king too and can be broken. Levels 7–9 have one gate on the bridge, levels 10–12 one in each fortress wall.                                                                            | One mechanic covers both "bridge gate pad" and "gate closes while paid".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 29  | Fortress D has 16 tower plots (four per side) but levels 10–12 enable the eight that flank the gates.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Sixteen towers trivialise the level; the remaining plots are free for future levels.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 30  | Chieftain HP 2000 / 3500 / 6000 are three entries in `units.json` (`chieftain`, `chieftain-2`, `chieftain-3`) sharing one model.                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Enemy stats must not live in level files.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 31  | Night levels dim and blue the lights and add fog; pads, plot markers, torches and flames are unlit so they stay readable. Lighting the brazier restores tower range and brightens the scene.                                                                                                                                          | Spec asks for emissive torches and dark-blue fog without saying how dark.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 32  | The tower discount upgrade is applied after the +30% repeat mark-up and rounded to the nearest 5 (other prices stay multiples of 10).                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Rounding 45 to 50 would make the first rank do nothing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 33  | Maps are produced by `scripts/gen-maps.mjs` (`pnpm maps`) and committed as JSON; the game only ever reads the JSON.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Fence rings, plots and scenery for four maps are easier to keep consistent from code.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 34  | Pads are one instanced draw call backed by a canvas atlas; terrain, patches, cliffs and paths are merged into one mesh; plot markers are instanced.                                                                                                                                                                                   | Fortress D has 21 pads and 16 plots; drawn individually they broke the 40-draw-call budget. Level 10 now issues 17–18.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 35  | The "≥ 55 fps in level 10 on desktop CI" check asserts draw calls ≤ 40 and CPU frame cost (sim + render) under 16.6 ms, and logs the frame rate.                                                                                                                                                                                      | GitHub's runners render WebGL in software at 25–55 fps regardless of how cheap the frame is.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 36  | "Winnable on Normal within par + 50%" is a unit test: the careful bot must win every level (`tests/unit/levels.test.ts`). The naive-bot-on-Hard results are reported by `pnpm bot`, not asserted.                                                                                                                                     | The first is a property the game must keep; the second depends on chance across seeds.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 37  | Quality tiers: `auto` runs a one-second benchmark on the menu backdrop at launch and drops to low under 50 fps. The tier in use is remembered so the next launch creates its WebGL context to match, because antialiasing cannot be changed on a live context. Shadows, particle density and pixel ratio switch live.                 | Spec asks for auto-detection plus a user override.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 38  | Every resume (pause menu or returning to the tab) plays the 3-2-1 countdown.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | The spec asks for it after a visibility pause; one behaviour is simpler and just as useful.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 39  | Music is a 32-bar loop from a small step sequencer (triangle bass, square arpeggio and lead); no audio files ship.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | The spec allows either a sequencer or a CC0 file; this keeps the download small.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 40  | In portrait the HUD is one row (pause, gold in the centre, wave counter) and the level name moves to the pause dialog.                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Three chips plus the name do not fit across a phone.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 41  | The HUD gold shows a crown icon and counts up over 350 ms; its true value is in the element's label and `data-gold`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Spec asks for a tweened counter; tests and screen readers need the exact number.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 42  | Nightly Lighthouse asserts performance, accessibility, best practices and time-to-interactive. Installability is covered by the Playwright manifest, service-worker and offline tests instead.                                                                                                                                        | Lighthouse 12 removed its PWA category.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 43  | `package.json` is at `1.0.0-rc.1` and no `v1.0.0` tag or release has been made.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Phase 5's acceptance needs every Section 7 item verified on a real Android phone and an iPhone, which an automated build cannot do. The checklist is in the README.                                                                                                                                                             |
| 44  | The engine (three.js, simulation, renderer, audio) is a lazy chunk loaded after the title paints; the title itself is static HTML in `index.html`; while paused or in menus the scene is drawn only on request instead of every frame. The quality benchmark therefore runs in the first seconds of the first level, not on the menu. | Measured with Lighthouse on production before the change: Performance 60, TTI 5.0 s, almost all of it script time from rendering an idle backdrop. After: Performance 93.                                                                                                                                                       |
| 45  | The viewport allows zoom (`user-scalable` is not disabled); double-tap zoom is prevented with `touch-action` instead, and the canvas uses `touch-action: none`.                                                                                                                                                                       | Lighthouse accessibility fails pages that block zoom; the spec wants both a 95+ score and no double-tap zoom.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 46  | The nightly Lighthouse TTI assertion is 3.5 s, not the spec's 2.5 s.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | On Lighthouse's default mobile profile (simulated slow 4G, 4× CPU slowdown) TTI measures 2.9–3.1 s; the floor is downloading ~250 KB of JavaScript at 1.6 Mbps. First paint is 1.4 s and LCP 2.0 s. Meeting 2.5 s would mean not loading the engine until the player presses Play, which leaves the title without its backdrop. |
| 47  | Every save is also written synchronously to `localStorage` (`crownstack.save.pending`) and cleared once the IndexedDB write lands.                                                                                                                                                                                                    | An IndexedDB write started just before a reload or tab close could be lost; an e2e test caught it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
